Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026. Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DevToolsHive ("we", "us", "our" or "Company") collects, uses and shares your information when you use our website and tools.

1. Introduction

DevToolsHive operates on a privacy-first principle. Our tools are designed to process your data directly in your browser, which means your information never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices in detail.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Browser-Based Tool Usage

DevToolsHive tools are executed entirely in your browser. When you use tools such as JSON Formatter, JWT Decoder, Base64 Encoder or UUID Generator, all data processing happens on your device. We do not collect or store:

  • The content you input into our tools.
  • The results generated by our tools.
  • Any transformations or conversions you perform.
  • Tokens, keys, credentials or sensitive data you work with.

2.2 Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve our tools and identify popular features. Google Analytics collects aggregate, non-personal metrics including:

  • Page views and visitor counts.
  • Traffic sources and referral data.
  • Device types and browser information.
  • Approximate geographic location (country or region level).
  • Interaction patterns and user flow.

This data is collected in aggregate form and may include pseudonymous identifiers used by Google Analytics. Google Analytics does not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using Google's opt-out browser extension.

2.3 Cookies

DevToolsHive uses both essential cookies and third-party cookies that may be set by services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense. We use cookies to:

  • Maintain your preferred theme (dark/light mode).
  • Remember your language preference.
  • Store temporary session data necessary for tool functionality.

These cookies are first-party cookies stored locally in your browser. They do not contain personal information and are not shared with third parties. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, although this may affect tool functionality.

2.4 Contact Form Data

If you contact us through our Contact Us page or email, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, which may include your name, email address, subject and message content. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not use this information for marketing or sell it to third parties.

2.5 Server Logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) maintains server logs that include your IP address, browser type, operating system, pages accessed and access times. These logs are used for security, abuse prevention and system administration purposes. We do not directly access or use this data for any other purpose.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for limited, specific purposes:

  • Service Delivery — To provide our tools and website functionality.
  • Analytics — To understand traffic patterns and improve our services through Google Analytics.
  • Communication — To respond to your inquiries and support requests.
  • Security — To detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse and security issues.
  • Legal Compliance — To comply with applicable laws, regulations and legal requests.

4. Google AdSense

DevToolsHive may display Google AdSense advertisements on our website. Google AdSense has its own privacy practices and may collect information to serve contextually relevant ads. For information about Google's advertising practices and how to opt out, visit Google's Ads Policies.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — Website analytics and traffic reporting.
  • Google AdSense — Ad serving and revenue (future).
  • GitHub Pages — Website hosting and infrastructure.

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their privacy practices. We are not responsible for their data collection or handling practices.

6. Data Retention

Since DevToolsHive tools do not store user data on our servers, there is no retention period for tool usage data. Cookies and theme preferences are stored locally in your browser indefinitely until you clear your browser data or manually delete them. Contact inquiry data is retained as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and may be retained for record-keeping purposes.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, lease or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties. We do not share user data except:

  • As required by law, court order or legal process.
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety or property and that of our users.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of DevToolsHive.
  • As part of aggregated, anonymized analytics shared with our service providers.

8. Your Privacy Rights

8.1 General Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you.
  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to our use of your personal information.

If you are located in the European Union, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including:

  • Right of access to your personal data.
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restrict processing.
  • Right to data portability.
  • Right to object to processing.
  • Rights related to automated decision-making and profiling.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at officialmail316@gmail.com.

8.3 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) including:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used and shared.
  • Right to delete personal information.
  • Right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

We do not sell or share personal information. To exercise your CCPA rights, please contact us at officialmail316@gmail.com.

9. Security

We take security seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. Since our tools operate in your browser, your data is protected by your device's security. However, no security system is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use strong passwords and keep your device secure.

10. Children's Privacy

DevToolsHive is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

11. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, stored in and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your home country. By using DevToolsHive, you consent to such transfers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Your continued use of DevToolsHive following such updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
